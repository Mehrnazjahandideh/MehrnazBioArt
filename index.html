About Me

Web Developer and Designer

My name is Mehrnaz Jahandideh, I am 26 years old and I hold a bachelor’s degree in Psychology from Iran. After graduation, I worked in an online store where I became more familiar with technology, artificial intelligence, and programming. This experience encouraged me to start learning Python and later move toward web development with Django. Alongside this path, art and drawing have always been an important part of my life. While art and programming may seem like two completely different worlds to many people, for me they both mean the same thing: creating. Creating has always been more than just an interest to me; it is something that gives meaning to my life and direction to my path. Today, I am trying to build a connection between art and technology by using programming as a tool for creating interactive and creative experiences. In the future, I would like to explore concept art, animation, and digital media more deeply while continuing to develop my technical skills with Python. My goal is to build a bridge between artists and developers, combining creativity and technology to create projects where art and code can exist together.
